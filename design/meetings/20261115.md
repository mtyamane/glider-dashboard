Mark used both ChatGPT and Claude, finding 10 and 12 use cases respectively, with significant overlap across all three members' results.

## Clustering Exercise (led by Danner)

The team split into breakout groups to identify similar and unique user stories, merging overlapping ones.

Final clusters identified:
1. View Data – centralized platform for past and current deployment data
2. Ocean Trends – zooplankton biomass over space/time with environmental context
3. Accessible/User-Friendly – public-facing, not just for scientists
4. Deployment Summary – biological and technical data summaries per deployment
5. Data Access – securely pull/download data via API
6. Sampling – assist with sampling/deployment decisions
