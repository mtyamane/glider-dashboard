The team discussed categorizing user stories for a glider deployment system, focusing on grouping similar features together. They identified four main groups: adding optical data products, visualizing data types, providing deployment summaries, and making the system user-agnostic. The team worked to pair remaining cards and identified three additional distinct features: monitoring glider battery levels, accessing/downloading data through APIs, and mapping seawater pollution. Mark explained the concept of plankton blooms and crashes to help with categorization. ### Glider Data Tool Requirements Discussion

Danner and Mark discussed key features users need from their glider data tool, identifying five main requirements: a centralized platform to view deployment data, analysis of zooplankton biomass over space and time with environmental context, user-friendly and accessible interface for non-scientists, summaries of biological and technical data per deployment, and secure data download capabilities. They agreed the tool should also assist with sampling decisions. Mark planned to add a screenshot to their documentation and refine the presentation format by breaking down longer descriptions into shorter cards with 3-5 key words each.
